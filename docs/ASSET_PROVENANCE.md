# Asset provenance

## Cocos combat sound effects

- Source: generated locally by `scripts/generate-cocos-audio.mjs`.
- Inputs: deterministic oscillator, transient and noise formulas authored in this repository.
- External assets or services: none.
- Usage: unrestricted within Project Expedition and its Web, App Store and Steam builds.
- Files: `apps/game-client-cocos/assets/resources/audio/*.wav`.
